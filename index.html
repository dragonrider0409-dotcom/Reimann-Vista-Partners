        Questions

        Frequently asked questions.

        What comes in a box?

        Every box holds 12 sachets of 60g (2.1oz) each, and each sachet has 25g of carbohydrates. A single box is one flavor.

        What is the difference between the two lines?

        The no caffeine line has none. The caffeinated line adds 100mg of caffeine to every sachet, on top of the same 25g of carbohydrates. Caffeinated gels are not intended for children, or for anyone who is pregnant, nursing or sensitive to caffeine.

        Which flavors are there?

        Four: Blood Orange Yuzu, White Peach Raspberry, Pineapple Passion Fruit and Mixed Berry. Each comes with or without caffeine.

        Is shipping really free?

        Yes. Shipping is built into every price, so the price you see on the page is the price you pay.

        What if I want to return an order?

        Return an order and you get a full refund. The returns and refunds page has the details.

        How does the monthly plan work?

        A box or trio arrives every month and the plan price is charged each month until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in your account's subscription tab. Coupons are issued once at each monthly reset. Cancelling does not restore coupons you have used, and resubscribing does not give you a new set until the next monthly reset.

        How do the coupons work?

        Every monthly payment includes three 20% off coupons. Use them on single and triple box purchases at the regular price. They do not apply to monthly plans or their renewals.

        Can I mix flavors in a trio?

        Yes. Pick any three boxes from the four flavors in the no caffeine or the caffeinated line. Use one flavor three times, or mix them. Buy the trio once or have it arrive every month.
